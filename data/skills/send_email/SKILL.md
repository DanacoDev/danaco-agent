description: Sends email from this project's mailbox. args to, subject, body, optional account.
version: 1.0.0

args to, subject, body. Optional account is the mailbox label when this project has more than one and you must choose. The mailbox is who the message is from. to is who it is written to. Never put your own From address in to. If you do not know who should receive it, ask. Do not email your own address.

Call this when mail should go out. Do not delegate a send, and do not say the skill is missing. If this chat has no mailbox, say that a mailbox is added on the Setup page, under Email, and linked to this project.
